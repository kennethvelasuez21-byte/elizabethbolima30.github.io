About Me

I started my professional journey at 20 years old in April 2020 as a Service Crew at Jollibee Burgos Batangas. Within a few months, I was accepted as a Technical Support Specialist in October of 2020. My role there was to troubleshoot and assist customers with their technical problems, fix devices such as printers, and handle sales by offering warranties to printers and subscriptions to their inks. If products were out of warranty and unfixable due to age, I also guided customers toward purchasing new printers to ensure continuity.

After 3 years of working in technical support, I sought new opportunities and transitioned to TaskUs in February 2024 as a Customer Service Specialist handling order placement, deliveries, statuses, and quality management. Up until now, I am continuing my excellence at TaskUs while exploring new growth paths. Across these roles, I have developed hands-on expertise in customer operations, administrative support, invoicing, client communication, e-commerce order management, and CRM systems.
